## Passive buzzer

## Case (optional)

The reader uses the same wiring as the [tagreader](https://github.com/adonno/tagreader) project. Its printable case is in the [STLs](https://github.com/adonno/tagreader/tree/master/STLs) folder; it fits the D1 mini and the PN532. Without a 3D printer, a small plastic project box from any source works as well: just do not shield the PN532 antenna with metal, the card is read through a few millimetres of plastic. The case is not part of the sum.
